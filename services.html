Disease prevention

Information on the diseases reported most often in Malaysia and the actions that reduce their spread. Each guide covers the cause, how it spreads, warning signs, and prevention at home.

Covered topics

  • Dengue and Aedes control around the house
  • Tuberculosis and long-lasting coughs
  • Hand, foot and mouth disease in nurseries and kindergartens
  • Influenza and other respiratory infections
  • Food and water-borne illness, including food poisoning

Benefit

Readers learn which single action matters most for each disease instead of a long list they will not follow.

Health and hygiene tips

Everyday practices that break the chain of infection: handwashing with soap at the right moments, safe food handling and storage, clean drinking water, toilet and bathroom hygiene, and keeping shared spaces ventilated.

Benefit

These habits cost nothing and prevent a large share of common infections, especially in households with young children or older adults.

Vaccination awareness

Plain explanations of how vaccines train the immune system, what the childhood immunisation schedule covers, which vaccines adults may still need, and what the common side effects look like.

Benefit

Parents and adults get answers to the questions that usually cause hesitation, with links to the official schedule rather than rumours from social media.

Healthy lifestyle

Nutrition, physical activity, sleep and stress, written around local food and local routines. Practical swaps for hawker meals, portion guidance, simple exercise that needs no gym, and sleep habits for shift workers.

Benefit

Small, realistic changes that lower the risk of diabetes, high blood pressure and heart disease over time.

Early detection

Warning signs worth acting on, and which screenings to request at each stage of life: blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, body weight, and cancer screening where it applies.

Rule of thumb

A cough lasting more than two weeks, unexplained weight loss, a lump that does not go away, or blood where there should be none are all reasons to see a doctor without waiting.

Health education

Short articles, step-by-step guides and printable resources written for the general public. Teachers may use them in class, and community groups may reproduce them with credit.

Community awareness

Materials and simple activity plans for running an awareness session in a school, office or neighbourhood: poster templates, a talk outline, a checklist for a residential Aedes walkabout, and follow-up ideas.

Invite us to run a session if your group would like help getting started.

Why use this portal

Easy to use

Short sections and plain sentences. Technical terms are explained the first time they appear.

Properly researched

Two reviewers per page, references listed, and a review date shown on every article.
