Before you start

Medical emergencies

This page answers questions about the portal and general health topics. It is not a triage service. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, fits or sudden weakness, call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

General

Is this portal a replacement for seeing a doctor?

No. Everything here is general health education. If you have symptoms, or something feels wrong, go to a clinic or hospital and speak to a medical professional. In an emergency call 999.

Do I need to pay or create an account?

No. The portal is free to read and share. There is no sign-up, no paywall and no advertising.

Who runs this website?

A student team based in Kuala Lumpur. Details of the members and their roles are on the About Us page.

Content and sources

Where does your information come from?

Public guidance issued by the Ministry of Health Malaysia and the World Health Organization, rewritten in plain language. References are listed at the bottom of each article.

How often is content updated?

Every page is reviewed at least once a semester, and sooner if official guidance changes. The review date appears on each page.

I found a mistake. How do I report it?

Use the contact form and point to the paragraph in question. Corrections are checked within a few working days and noted on the page.

Using our material

Can I use your content for a school or workplace campaign?

Yes, as long as you credit Sihat Bersama and do not change the meaning of the health advice. Ask us if you need a printable version.

Can you speak at our school, mosque or office?

We run sessions where our schedule allows. Send the date, location and expected audience size through the contact form.

Can we translate your pages?

Yes, with credit. Send us the translated version so we can check that the health advice was not changed during translation.

Privacy

What data does this site collect?

Only what you type into the contact form. We use it to reply to you and nothing else. We do not sell or share it.

Do you use cookies or tracking?

The portal itself does not set tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from an external provider, which may log the request as any web request is logged.
